## Turning a New Release Into Muscle Memory

Most "broken controls" complaints are actually aim complaints. The binds work; the stroke does not. Stand close enough that the washer cone or squeegee blade clearly intersects the glass. If you are too far away, the tool still fires, but you spend the pass on air. Square your body to the pane, then rotate so the blade's long edge matches the pane's long edge.

Work the edges on purpose. Center-of-pane spamming leaves a dirty frame that a finishing check will catch. Start a squeegee stroke at one gasket and pull through to the opposite gasket, overlapping each stroke slightly. If a window is taller than your blade, rotate and run vertical passes instead of dabbing at it horizontally.

| Drill | What to do | What it fixes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Washer field | Pick one dirty wall, use only the washer, skip the shop | Overlap rhythm and trigger discipline |
| Gasket to gasket | Full squeegee strokes edge to edge without stopping mid-pane | Dirty frames and smeared corners |
| Side-on check | After each pass, look along the glass instead of straight at it | Streaks that are invisible head-on |

Ten focused minutes of drills is worth an hour of confused climbing. Once the inputs feel automatic, set a real session goal, invite a friend, and split washer duty from squeegee duty. That is how the Wash The Skyscraper release date stops mattering and the actual game starts.
